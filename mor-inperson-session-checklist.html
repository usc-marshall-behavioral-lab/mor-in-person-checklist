Before you go · The day before

Get ready

  • Know your SONA ID. It is your Identity Code, the 5- or 6-digit number in your SONA profile. Screenshot it or write it down. You'll need it at check-in. Bring your confirmation email too.
  • Confirm your session date and time on SONA. Check the day before. Sessions cannot accommodate late arrivals.
  • Know the location. VPD lower level, Verna & Peter Dauterive Hall. Your session is in LL102, LL110, or LL112. 635 Downey Way, Los Angeles, CA 90089. Find the Lab · USC Campus Map · Google Maps
  • Only water is allowed in the lab. No other food or drinks. Eat before you arrive. Sessions last the full scheduled time.
When you arrive · At the door

Check in

Arrive on time. Doors close after check-in. Can't find the lab? Text us.

  • Arrive at the scheduled time and ring the doorbell at your room. A researcher will greet you and check you in. You'll enter one by one.
  • Give the RA your SONA ID (5- or 6-digit number) so they can check you off the list. No ID? You can use your name + USC email instead.
  • Turn your phone completely off (not just silent) before you go in. If the researcher requests it, your phone goes in a locker and you keep the key until the end of the session.
  • Leave your bag in the reception area before you go in.
  • Sit at the assigned seat. The RA will direct you. Don't choose your own. Friends are seated separately to preserve research quality.
  • Don't touch the computer until the RA says to begin. Everyone starts together.
During the session · At your station

Take the studies

Follow RA instructions throughout.

  • Complete studies in the order the RA gives you. The RA manages the study order for research accuracy.
  • When you finish a task, raise your hand or open the door. Which one depends on your room, and the RA will tell you. An assistant will then tell you what to do next.
  • Read instructions carefully and answer honestly. There are no right or wrong answers. In-person studies are usually computer tasks. Some involve group activities or interviews.
  • If you finish early, stay quiet. You can browse the web on the lab computer while you wait.
  • You cannot leave early. The session ends at the scheduled time for everyone.
After the session · On your way out

Wrap up

  • Pick up your bag from the reception area. If your phone was in a locker, collect it and return your key.
  • Credit posts within 24 hours of the session. Check your SONA profile to confirm. If your credit doesn't appear, email mor.sona@marshall.usc.edu.
  • Don't discuss study content with classmates or other students. This protects research validity.
Key policies · Rules to know

Good to know

  • Credit depends on how long you are in the lab. 0.25 credit per 15 minutes, 0.5 for about 30 minutes, 1.0 up to 60 minutes, and 1.5 up to 90 minutes. Online and in-person studies earn the same credit. To see what has posted, check your SONA credit history.
  • Late arrivals cannot be admitted. All participants must start together for research validity, with no exceptions. Doors close shortly after the session time. Plan to arrive a few minutes early.
  • Cancel on SONA at least 6 hours ahead if you can't make it (illness, emergency, etc.). Cancelling early lets another student take your seat.
  • Your responses are confidential. They are never shared with your instructor or linked to your identity.
No preparation is needed for the studies themselves. Come on time with your SONA ID, and the research assistants will guide you through everything.
